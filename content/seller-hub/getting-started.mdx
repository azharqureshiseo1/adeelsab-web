If you have never sold anything online before, this is the page to read first. It assumes no prior experience and no website of your own.

## Before you register

You need four things, and you almost certainly already have all of them.

- **Your CNIC.** A clear photograph of both sides. This verifies the seller account and is required by law.
- **A payout account.** A bank account in your own name, or a JazzCash or Easypaisa number registered on your CNIC. The account title has to match your CNIC, otherwise payments will fail later.
- **Products you actually hold.** At least ten items you can send out within 24 hours of an order. Do not list things you would have to go and buy first.
- **A WhatsApp number you answer.** Almost everything that follows happens on WhatsApp.

You do **not** need a company, a website, an NTN, a shop lease, or any English.

## The onboarding call

After you send the form, somebody calls you in Urdu, usually within one working day. The call takes about fifteen minutes and covers:

1. **What you sell.** We agree your categories, because the commission you pay depends on them.
2. **Your pricing.** We check that your prices leave room for delivery cost and commission.
3. **Your dispatch.** Where the rider or courier collects from, and what hours you are there.
4. **Your payout details.** Bank or wallet, confirmed against your CNIC.

Ask everything you want to ask on this call. It is normal to ask the same question three times, and the person on the phone is expecting that.

## Your first listings

You have two options, and most new sellers take the second.

**Do it yourself.** You add each product through the seller panel: photographs, title, description, price, stock quantity and weight. There is a guide on [how to list products](/seller-hub/how-to-list-products).

**Send us the photographs.** You WhatsApp us your product pictures, and we write the titles, descriptions and specifications and build the listings for you. This is free, and for Founding Sellers it stays free. You approve everything before it goes live.

Either way, [take decent photographs](/seller-hub/product-photography-with-a-phone) first. A phone camera and a window are enough, and the quality of the photograph is the single biggest factor in whether anything sells.

## Your first order

When an order comes in you get a WhatsApp message and a notification in the panel. Then:

1. **Confirm the stock is there.** If it is not, cancel immediately rather than letting the order sit. A cancellation is recoverable; a silent delay is not.
2. **Pack it properly.** See the [packaging guide](/seller-hub/packaging-guide). Damaged goods come back to you and you pay the return charge.
3. **Mark it ready.** The rider or courier is dispatched once you do this.
4. **Hand it over.** Keep the receipt or the scan confirmation until the order shows as delivered.

Most first-time sellers get an order out in under twenty minutes once they have done it twice.

## Getting paid

Cash on delivery is collected by us, not by you. Card payments come to us too. Either way, the money reaches your account on the standard settlement cycle after delivery is confirmed, with commission, any shipping you chose to absorb, and any return charges deducted as separate lines. The full detail is on the [payouts page](/payouts).

## Common mistakes in week one

**Listing stock you do not have.** The most expensive mistake available. Cancelled orders lose you money and ranking, and lose the buyer permanently.

**Guessing the weight.** Shipping is charged by weight band. Weigh a packed parcel on a kitchen scale once per product and record it; guessing means you are either overcharging customers or absorbing the difference yourself.

**Photographing against a patterned background.** A bedsheet with a pattern on it makes a product look second-hand. Plain wall, plain floor, daylight.

**Not answering WhatsApp.** Buyers who cannot get an answer refuse the parcel at the door, and then you pay the return charge. Answering within a few hours removes most of this.

**Pricing without the delivery cost.** Work out your price with commission and shipping included, before you list. If the margin does not work at that price, the product is not ready to list.
